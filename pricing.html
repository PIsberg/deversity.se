One license covers your whole team — there are no per-seat keys. The developer count is the number of developers whose builds run async-test-lib. Subscriptions renew yearly and can be cancelled at any time; a cancelled license stays valid until the end of the paid term.

Your license is bound to one email address that you nominate — typically a shared address such as licence@yourcompany.com. Every developer and CI job uses that same address in the build config; they do not use their own. Tell us if it ever needs to move and we will re-point the license without issuing a new key.

Checkout is handled by Paddle, our merchant of record, which acts as the seller of record and handles VAT and sales tax. Prices exclude tax; it is added at checkout. Your license key is sent by email after purchase — it is issued by hand, so please allow one business day.

Prices are shown in your local currency where Paddle supports it; you are charged the amount displayed at checkout. Prefer an invoice, a purchase order, or have a question first? Email us.

Running without a license

Non-commercial use is free and needs no key. Developers on free email providers are treated as non-commercial users. CI systems and local development can run in mock mode. See the project documentation for details.
