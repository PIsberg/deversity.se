Deversity

Developer tooling for concurrency testing on the JVM.

Deversity builds and maintains async-test-lib, a JUnit 5 extension that stress-tests code under maximum thread contention. A single @AsyncTest annotation runs a test body on N threads for M rounds, forces the threads to collide, and reports what more than a hundred concurrency detectors observed: race conditions, deadlocks, missed signals, unsafe publication, and more.

How licensing works

async-test-lib is free for non-commercial use under the PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0. Commercial use requires an annual license, priced by team size. A license covers every developer on your company email domain. There are no per-seat keys.
